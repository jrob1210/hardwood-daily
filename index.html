How to play

Roots: Five players. Name the college each one last attended. If he skipped college, it's his home country, or No College if he's American (like LeBron). Miss and you get four choices.

Common Ground: Two players share a school or country. Name it. Three puzzles a day.

Pipeline: A mystery school or country is revealed when you start the clock. Name as many current players from there as you can in 60 seconds.

Rookie or Veteran: Rookie sticks to starters. Veteran uses full rosters. Pipeline is the same in both.
